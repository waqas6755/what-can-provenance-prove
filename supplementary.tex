\subsection*{Inter-rater agreement (to be completed)}
Two human screeners code an agreed random sample (at least 20\% of title/abstract records, and all full texts if feasible) independently, blind to each other and to the AI decisions. Report Cohen's $\kappa$ for the binary include/exclude decision with its 95\% confidence interval, the number of disagreements, and how they were adjudicated. The agreement between the AI decisions and the adjudicated human decisions may be reported separately, as a property of the AI screener, never as inter-rater agreement. \textbf{No agreement statistic is reported in this revision, because no second human screening has yet taken place.}
